Air Pollution & Public Health

Over 90% of the world’s population lives in areas where air quality exceeds WHO guideline limits. Air pollution is linked to asthma, cardiovascular diseases, stroke, and even premature death.

In urban areas, transportation and industrial emissions are primary contributors. In rural areas, indoor cooking with solid fuels adds to the burden. Read how nations like Sweden and Japan have tackled this challenge through clean energy initiatives and public awareness.

Plastic Pollution in Oceans

Every year, around 8 million metric tons of plastic enter the oceans. From microplastics to fishing nets, marine life is choking on our waste. Learn about the Pacific Garbage Patch and what organizations like Ocean Cleanup are doing to combat it.

Climate Change and Global Impact

The global temperature has already increased by over 1°C since pre-industrial times. Ice caps are melting, sea levels are rising, and weather patterns are changing drastically.

This article explores the science behind climate change, recent climate agreements like the Paris Accord, and how citizen action can shape government policy.

Water Pollution: A Hidden Threat

Contaminated water sources affect over 2 billion people globally. Industrial runoff, agricultural pesticides, and plastic waste all contribute to the degradation of rivers and oceans.
