Our Story

Founded in 2025, Pollution Awareness Blog began as a student-led initiative. Today, it's a growing platform for activists, researchers, and everyday citizens. We believe that education is the first step to environmental change.

What Drives Us

We believe that no effort is too small. Every act of awareness spreads ripples of change. By sharing facts, solutions, and inspiring stories, we aim to empower people to act against pollution in their own lives.
