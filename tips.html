Daily Habits for a Greener Life

  • Shop sustainably: Avoid overpackaged goods. Look for items made from recycled or biodegradable materials.
  • Embrace minimalism: Buy only what you need. Fewer products = less waste.
  • Switch to green power: Choose renewable energy providers when possible.
  • Reduce meat consumption: The livestock industry is a major contributor to methane and deforestation.

Eco-Home Upgrades

  • Install LED lighting: Save energy and reduce utility bills.
  • Use natural cleaning products: Vinegar, baking soda, and lemon are great alternatives to harsh chemicals.
  • Set up rainwater harvesting: Use collected rainwater for gardening.
  • Install solar panels: Reduce reliance on fossil fuels and gain long-term energy savings.

For Students & Schools

Start eco clubs, organize clean-up drives, and encourage recycling programs in your school or college. Education and early action build lifelong habits.
